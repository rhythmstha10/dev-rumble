One platform, three parts

01 — LIBRARY

Search, borrow, track

Search the catalog, borrow and return books, see due dates and fines, and review what you've read — all built on the existing library system.

02 — CAMPUS AI

Ask, don't search

"What books do I currently have?" "When is my DBMS book due?" Campus AI answers using your real borrowing records — not guesses.

03 — STUDY

Recommendations & study plans

Get book recommendations based on what you've borrowed, and a day-by-day study plan built around your exam date.

How Campus AI stays honest

The AI never touches the database directly. It only ever sees the same records you're allowed to see.

1. You ask"When is my Python book due?"
2. Backend looks it upDjango retrieves your actual BorrowRecord — nothing more.
3. AI gets contextOnly the relevant book, dates, and status are passed to the model.
4. You get an answerA plain-language reply, grounded in your real data.
